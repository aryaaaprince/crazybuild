  • High tensile strength & ductility
  • Superior earthquake resistance
  • Corrosion resistant ribbing

JSW Neosteel

TMT · Fe 550D
  • Fine-grain, low carbon core
  • Excellent weldability
  • Consistent cross-section, less wastage

Tata Tiscon

TMT · Fe 500
  • Uniform elongation, high bendability
  • Rust & fire resistant coating
  • Trusted for high-rise structures

SAIL TMT

TMT · Fe 500
  • Government-grade mill certification
  • High load-bearing capacity
  • Reliable for foundation & columns
