Where it actually comes from

How We Source.
What We Check.

Every material on this page is traced back to a real quarry, mill or kiln before it's listed — not just picked off a price sheet.

01

Direct from source

Steel comes straight from mill-certified brands, sand and stone from named quarry regions like Tosham, Narnaul, Dadri and Atella — no unnamed middlemen reselling unknown stock.

02

Checked before listing

Our verification desk confirms a retailer's actual stock and rate before their listing goes live — a listed price is a price you can call and expect to get.

03

Graded, not guessed

Every material carries its real grade — Fe 500D steel, RCC-grade grit, fine vs coarse sand — so you know exactly what you're buying, not just a category name.
